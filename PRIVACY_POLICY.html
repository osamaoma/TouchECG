Privacy Policy for TouchECG Effective date: October 29, 2026 Developer: osamaomer Contact: omer.osama@gmail.com TouchECG measures your pulse with your phone's rear camera and flash and shows an estimated heart rate and pulse waveform. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: the app works entirely on your phone, collects no personal data, and sends nothing to us or to anyone else. 1. Information the app uses Camera images. When you press Measure, the app uses the rear camera with the flash on for about 35 seconds. For each video frame, it calculates the average green brightness of the image. The frame images themselves are not saved, shown, or transmitted. Only the resulting numbers are kept in memory while the measurement runs. Measurement results. The heart rate and the estimated pulse waveform are shown on your screen. They are held in memory only while the app is open and are discarded when you close the app or start a new measurement. The app does not store them on your device or anywhere else. 2. Processing on your device All processing happens on your phone: signal reconstruction, peak detection, heart rate calculation, and the neural network that estimates the pulse waveform. The network is included in the app. No measurement data is sent to a server, to the developer, or to any third party. 3. Information we do not collect TouchECG does not collect, store, share, or sell: personal information such as your name, email address, or account details (the app has no accounts); health records or measurement history; device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or files; usage analytics, crash reports sent to a service, or advertising identifiers. The app does not request internet access and contains no advertising or analytics libraries. 4. Permissions Camera (`android.permission.CAMERA`): needed to measure the pulse. The app asks for it the first time you press Measure. The camera is only active during a measurement and is switched off afterwards. Flash: used as a light source during a measurement. It stays on for the whole measurement and turns off when the camera is released. You can revoke camera permission at any time in your phone's settings. The app will then be unable to measure. 5. Third-party components The app includes open-source libraries that run on your device: Android CameraX (camera access) and ONNX Runtime (running the neural network). These libraries do not send data anywhere when used by this app. 6. Children TouchECG is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone. 7. Medical disclaimer TouchECG is a research prototype and is not a medical device. Its heart rate and waveform estimates are not validated for diagnosis, treatment, or monitoring. Do not rely on it for medical decisions. If you have chest pain, an irregular or fast heartbeat, shortness of breath, dizziness, or other symptoms, contact a doctor or emergency services. 8. Security Because the app keeps no stored data and sends nothing over the network, there is no measurement data to protect beyond your device's own security. Keep your phone's operating system and screen lock up to date. 9. Changes to this policy If the app's behaviour changes, this policy will be updated and the effective date revised. Material changes that add data collection will be announced in the app before they take effect. 10. Contact Questions about this policy: omer.osama@gmail.com.
